Do you offer a price match?

Yes — we offer a Price Match Guarantee. If you find the same part for less somewhere else, call us at (832) 205-8313 or email us and we'll do our best to match it. See our Price Match Guarantee page for full details.

What are your shipping rates?

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your location and the size of your order, and we offer free shipping on many items — look for the Free Shipping badge on the product page. See our Shipping Info page for full details.

What's my order status?

You can check your order status anytime from your account under My Orders, or use the tracking link we email you once your order ships. Still have questions? Call (832) 205-8313 or email us at customerservice@chromeworldtrucks.com.

If I place an order, what is my lead time?

Most in-stock items ship within 24–48 hours. If an item isn't in stock, it typically ships in 1–4 weeks, depending on the manufacturer and where it's shipping from.

Where can I do a product inquiry, or get more details about a product?

Email us at customerservice@chromeworldtrucks.com or call (832) 205-8313 — our team is happy to answer any product questions.

Will I get a warranty on my purchase?

Yes — products are covered based on the manufacturer's warranty limit. Warranty details are listed on each product's description page. Please note: damage caused by road hazards is not covered under warranty.

I want to buy a product that isn't on the website — can you help?

While we carry many products that aren't listed on the website, you can always call or email us — we can find the product for you, and custom orders are possible too.

What should I do if my product gets damaged in transit?

Please refer to our Shipping Info page for the steps to take, or contact us directly and we'll take care of it right away.

Can I pick up my product in-store?

Yes! In-store pickup is available — just let us know when you order, or contact us to arrange a pickup time.
