We work hard to offer the best prices on chrome truck accessories and parts. If you find an identical, in-stock item for less at an authorized retailer, we'll match it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Contact us

    Reach out within 7 days of your purchase at customerservice@chromeworldtrucks.com or (832) 205-8313.

  2. 2

    Show us the price

    Send a link or screenshot of the competitor's current price for the exact same item — same brand, part number, and condition.

  3. 3

    We match it

    Once verified, we'll match the price or refund the difference on your order.

A few conditions

  • The competitor must be an authorized retailer with the item in stock — not a marketplace third-party seller, closeout, or auction listing.
  • Price match requests must be submitted within 7 days of purchase.
  • Excludes clearance, closeout, and open-box items.
