Return Policy

Most of our items can be returned for a refund within 30 days of delivery. If you miss that mark, don't worry — you can receive store credit any time after that, and it never expires. Shipping fees are non-refundable, and return shipping costs will apply. The following conditions must be met:

  • All items returned must be in their original, undamaged condition.
  • No dents, marks, or scratches, and no protective material removed.
  • Please disassemble the item if it was originally delivered disassembled.
  • Return it in its original packaging — a similar-sized box is fine if the original packaging is damaged.
  • All returns must be approved by our customer service team first.
  • Your order may be cancelled if requested before it ships.
  • Return shipping cost is deducted from the refund amount — unless the item arrived damaged or defective, in which case we'll work with you to find the best solution.

Once we receive your returned item, your refund will be issued to your original payment method. Please note: custom-made orders are not eligible for return.

Refund Options

You can choose how you'd like to be refunded once your return is received and approved:

  • Store Credit

    Never expires — use it whenever you're ready for your next order.

  • Original Payment Method

    Refunded to the card or account you paid with. Return shipping costs are deducted from the total.

Exchange Policy

Most items can be exchanged for store credit within 30 days of delivery, under the same conditions as a standard return. Custom-made orders are not eligible for exchange. We're continuing to improve our exchange process — thanks for your patience in the meantime.
