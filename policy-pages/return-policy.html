Return Policy At A Glance

  • 30-Day Full Refund

    Return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.

  • 365-Day Store Credit

    Past 30 days? You've got a full year from delivery to return it for store credit.

  • No Restocking Fees

    We don't charge restocking fees on eligible returns — ever.

Our return window runs a full 365 days (1 year) from the delivery date. Return within the first 30 days for a full refund to your original payment method, or any time within the year for store credit. Shipping fees are non-refundable, and return shipping costs will apply. Custom-made orders are not eligible for return.

Return Condition

Returns must be sent back in resellable condition. That means:

  • All items returned must be in their original, resellable condition.
  • No dents, marks, or scratches, and no protective material removed.
  • Please disassemble the item if it was originally delivered disassembled.
  • Return it in its original packaging — a similar-sized box is fine if the original packaging is damaged.
  • All returns must be approved by our customer service team first.
  • Return shipping cost is deducted from the refund amount — unless the item arrived damaged or defective, in which case we'll work with you to find the best solution.

Please note: we reserve the right to decline a refund if the returned item is damaged, used, or otherwise not in resellable condition.

Refund Options

You can choose how you'd like to be refunded once your return is received and approved:

  • Store Credit

    Available for returns made within 365 days (1 year) of delivery.

  • Original Payment Method

    Available for returns made within 30 days of delivery. Return shipping costs are deducted from the total.

Exchange Policy

Most items can be exchanged for store credit within 30 days of delivery, under the same conditions as a standard return. Custom-made orders are not eligible for exchange. We're continuing to improve our exchange process — thanks for your patience in the meantime.
